Introduction

Asheville is my favorite city. Ever since I was a kid I knew I loved the mountains. When I was growing up my grandparents lived in Blowing Rock, a town near Boone in the mountains of North Carolina. It's an absolutely beautiful place. Asheville has found its place as my favorite city for a couple of key reasons:

  • UNCA Campus

    I go to school at UNC Asheville. It's campus is awesome, it's one of my favorite places in Asheville. It has everything you could possibly want out of a college campus. Everything you need to get to is close together, and yet campus is vast. Genuinely there are some better sights on this college campus than some dedicated hiking trails. The Observatory is absolutely stunning.

  • The Blue Ridge Parkway

    The Blue Ridge Parkway is one of my favorite places on earth. I've been to a lot of beautiful places, but the Blue Ridge Parkway is by far the prettiest of them. Time and time again I bring my cameras out, and come home with pictures upon pictures of the beautiful scenery.

    • Conclusion

    All in all, Asheville is:
    • Beautiful
    • Diverse
    • & Bright
